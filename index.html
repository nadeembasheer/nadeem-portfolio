About

Information technology is the field I work in. I have supported enterprise users and infrastructure (Active Directory, Windows Server, Microsoft 365, networking and ticketing under ITIL), and I design and build websites alongside that. Security is where I specialise.

I work in cybersecurity and IT infrastructure, with hands-on experience in SIEM platforms (Wazuh, Splunk), IDS/IPS (Suricata), vulnerability assessment and penetration testing, log analysis and incident triage. My work follows the OWASP Top 10 and ITIL.

I correlate security events, investigate alerts such as failed authentication, brute-force activity and malicious requests, and work with other teams to cut the time it takes to detect and respond. I also use AI tools for log and anomaly analysis and penetration-testing support to speed up SOC workflows.
